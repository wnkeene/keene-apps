Privacy Policy

Asort

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Simple Summary: Asort does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal information. It sorts your Safari bookmarks on your Mac, and your bookmarks never leave your Mac. The only time Asort goes online is to check the Keene Apps website for updates.

Information Collection and Use

Asort is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect any personal information from users of the app.

Full Disk Access

Asort asks for Full Disk Access because macOS protects Safari's bookmarks file. Asort uses this access only to read and sort that one file. It does not open your documents, mail, messages, or any other data.

Data Storage

Asort keeps a few settings on your Mac: whether Auto-sort is on, whether bookmarks are sorted by name or address, and when it last checked for updates. Your bookmarks stay in Safari's own file on your Mac.

No Analytics or Tracking

Asort does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Third-party SDKs that collect data

No Account Required

Asort does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use the app.

Update Checks

About once a day, and whenever you click Check for Updates…, Asort downloads a small update list from the Keene Apps website (wnkeene.github.io). The request includes only Asort's version number. It does not include your bookmarks, settings, or any personal information. When an update is available, Asort asks you before installing it.

The Keene Apps website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may log the request, including your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or keep these logs.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any information, the app is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal information in the app to breach or share. Updates are signed, and Asort installs only updates that carry the Keene Apps signature.

Your Rights

There is no personal data to access, export, or delete. Uninstall Asort… in the app removes its settings, its Full Disk Access, any downloaded updates, and the app itself. Your bookmarks stay as they are.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new policy on this page with an updated effective date.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected. For Asort: No personal information is collected, sold, or shared.

GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, Asort complies with GDPR because we do not process any personal data.
