Tip Cows

Toy cows on a green field. Tap to tip them. Tip the herd.

About

Tip Cows fills the screen with toy Holstein cows. Tap one and it tips onto its side. Tip them all before they stand back up. There is no account and no network.

How to Play

  1. Open — The herd takes the whole screen.
  2. Tap — A cow tips onto its side.
  3. Tip the herd — Get them all down before they stand back up.
  4. Hop — A cow that hops can be tapped for a little spin or flip.

Features

  • Full-screen playset — Toy Holstein cows, edge to edge, on iPhone and iPad
  • Tip — Tap a cow onto its side
  • Herd — Tip them all before they stand back up
  • Yours alone — No account, no ads, no internet

Platform Support

  • iPhone — iOS 26.0+
  • iPad — iPadOS 26.0+
